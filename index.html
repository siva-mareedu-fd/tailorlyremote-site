How it works

From résumé to a shortlist in an evening

Upload once, set what you want, and get a ranked list you can trust, with the reasons shown.

01

Upload your résumé

Add your Word (.docx) résumé. We read it once to understand your skills and experience, and you can correct anything we got wrong.

02

Get a daily shortlist

Every evening, at the time you choose, you get new remote jobs that match your preferences, each with a fit score, the reasons, and whether candidates in India are accepted.

03

Tailor and apply

Review a line-by-line diff of the changes we propose, accept only what you like, download the .docx, then apply on the employer’s page yourself.

Features

Built for developers who read the details

Every score, flag and change comes with its evidence, so you can decide quickly and with confidence.

eligibility

Open to India, with evidence

Many “remote” jobs exclude India. We show whether each role accepts candidates from India and quote the line from the listing that says so.

explained

Matches with reasons

A fit score from 1 to 10, the skills that matched, the skills that are missing, and how sure we are.

diff

Résumé changes you approve

We edit your own .docx in place, so fonts and layout stay the same. Every proposed change is a diff you accept or reject.

ats

Estimated ATS match

See how well your résumé matches a job’s requirements before and after tailoring. It is an estimate, and real systems vary.

tracker

Application tracker

A simple board from saved to offer. Move cards yourself, and keep your tailored files with each job.

prep

Cover letters and interview prep

Generate a concise or confident cover letter, and prep notes that use only experience from your résumé.

Honest by design

It never invents facts about you

A tailored résumé is only useful if every line is true. These rules are built into the product.

Skills only when you confirm them

If a job asks for something missing from your résumé, we ask you. A skill is added only if you say you have it.

Numbers, employers and titles stay yours

Proposed changes that add new numbers or names are flagged, and they stay off until you approve them.

No auto-apply

We never submit applications for you. Apply opens the original listing, and you submit it yourself.

Estimates are labelled

The ATS match is an estimate and is marked as one. We do not promise interviews or offers.

Your data

Your résumé is yours

Plain rules about what happens to the document you upload.

  • Your résumé is stored privately and is never shown to other users.
  • To create matches and documents, résumé text is processed by an AI provider (Anthropic). We say so at sign-up and ask for your consent.
  • You can delete a résumé, or your whole account, at any time, and the files are removed.
  • Backups are encrypted, and access to the app is protected by authenticated sessions and rate limits.
FAQ

Questions people ask

Who is Tailorly Remote for?

Developers and other tech professionals based in India who want remote roles, including roles hiring worldwide. It focuses on jobs that accept candidates from India.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. It shortlists jobs and prepares your résumé and cover letter. You open the original listing and submit the application yourself.

Which résumé formats work?

Word (.docx). Editing the original file is how your formatting is preserved, so PDFs are not supported for upload.

How accurate is the ATS score?

It is an estimate of how well your résumé matches a specific job’s requirements, and it is labelled as one. Real applicant tracking systems differ.

How do I get access?

The product is in an invite-only beta. Email shiv.mareedu@tailorlyremote.com to ask for an invite.

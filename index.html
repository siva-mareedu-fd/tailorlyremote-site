Looking for remote work from India takes more reading than applying.

  • Jobs are scattered

    Remote roles are spread across job boards and hundreds of company career pages.

  • “Remote” has fine print

    Many listings hire only in the US or the EU. You find out after reading the whole posting.

  • Every job wants its own résumé

    Rewriting for each role takes an evening, and one careless edit breaks the layout.

How it works

Tailorly does the searching, sorting and drafting. You make every decision.

  1. Upload your résumé once

    Add your Word (.docx) résumé. It is read once to understand your skills and experience, and you can correct anything it gets wrong.

  2. Say what you want

    Choose India only, India plus global remote, or global remote only. Add your roles, pay range and the lowest match score you want to see.

  3. Get a daily shortlist

    New jobs are ranked by fit and emailed at the time you pick, 18:00 IST unless you change it. Each one says why it fits and what is missing.

  4. Tailor, apply, track

    Approve résumé changes one by one, add a cover letter, and apply on the original job page. Interview prep and a tracking board come with it.

What makes it different

Three things matter most when you apply from India, and each one comes with its evidence.

It checks whether India is included

Every remote job is sorted into one of four groups, and you see the line from the listing that the decision rests on. Jobs that don’t say either way are marked unclear, with a warning, so you can check before you apply.

  • WorldwideThe employer hires anywhere.
  • Open to IndiaIndia or its region is named.
  • RestrictedLimited to other countries.
  • UnclearThe listing does not say.
“Open to candidates in APAC time zones.”The supporting line, quoted from a sample listing

It shows the score and what is behind it

For any job, you get an estimated match between your résumé and the posting, from 0 to 100, broken into its parts and shown before and after tailoring. It is called an estimate because real applicant tracking systems differ.

It alters your own file and adds nothing untrue

Changes are made to a copy of your Word file, so the fonts, spacing and layout stay exactly as you set them. A skill the job asks for is added only after you confirm you have it, and any edit that introduces something new is held for your review.

Never changed

  • Name and contact details
  • Employers and job titles
  • Dates
  • Degrees and certifications
  • Section headings

Can be reworded

  • Summary
  • Experience bullet points
  • Skills lines
  • Projects

Nothing is sent, changed or applied without you.

Tailorly drafts and suggests. Every change is yours to accept or reject, and every application is yours to send.

  • It never applies for you. Apply opens the original job page, and you mark the job as applied yourself.
  • You review every edit. The original and proposed wording sit side by side, with the reason for each change.
  • You can bring your own job. Paste a description you found on LinkedIn, Naukri or a company site and get the same score and tailoring.
  • You can delete everything. Deleting your account removes your résumés, tailored documents and tracker.

How your résumé is handled

Your résumé is stored privately and is never shown to other users. Downloads work only from your own signed-in account.

To create matches and documents, your résumé text and the jobs you analyse are sent to an AI provider, Anthropic, for processing. This is explained at sign-up, and nothing is processed until you agree.

Where the jobs come from

Questions

Does Tailorly apply to jobs for me?

No. Applying is always your action. The Apply button opens the original job page, and you mark the job as applied yourself.

How do you decide a job is open to India?

Where a source provides country or time-zone details, those are used. Otherwise the listing text is checked for limits such as “US only” or “must reside in”. You see the supporting line, and a job that cannot be settled is marked unclear.

Which résumé formats work?

Word (.docx) only. Tailoring edits a copy of your original file so that the formatting is kept, which is not possible with a PDF.

Will it add skills I don’t have?

No. If a job needs a skill that is missing from your résumé, you are asked whether you have it. Only skills you confirm can be used.

Can I use jobs I find elsewhere?

Yes. Paste the job description, with a link if you like, and it goes through the same scoring and tailoring. The link is saved for your reference and is never opened by Tailorly.

Is the ATS match score exact?

No. It estimates how well your résumé matches a job’s keywords and requirements. Real applicant tracking systems differ, so use it to find gaps, not as a promise.

What else do I get besides a tailored résumé?

A cover letter in a concise or a confident tone, interview preparation built only from your real experience, and a board to track each application from saved to offer.
